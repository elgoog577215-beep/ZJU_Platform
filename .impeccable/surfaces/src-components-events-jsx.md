# Activity Collection

- Scope: `/events` activity discovery, including category, audience, organization and sort filters, card/list views, AI assistant, publishing and event detail.
- Mode: Operate.
- Audience: students and campus visitors looking for the next activity, competition, volunteer opportunity or project call.
- Job: narrow a large activity feed, compare trustworthy event facts and open the right detail without losing filter context.
- Primary action: browse and filter activities; publishing remains a distinct authenticated action.
- Proof/content: real event imagery, title, lifecycle, date, location, organizer, audience and participation rewards.
- Constraints: preserve existing filter, sort, organization, AI assistant, upload, detail, pagination and URL behavior; support zh/en, day/night and 390px without horizontal overflow.
- Direction: use the main site's refined operational language without geometry dogma. Preserve the incumbent page background, dynamic wallpaper and restrained atmosphere instead of applying a page-wide blue wash. Give borders and solid surfaces only to controls or content that need separation; never wrap the filter area in an additional decorative container. Use micro-rounded neutral controls, a thin bordered container around the card/list switch, the incumbent blue underline for its current item, solid event entries and restrained hover depth. Avoid glass, glow, gradient cards and container-on-container hierarchy.
- Memorable moment: card/list switching reads instantly through the familiar icon-and-label rhythm, crisp blue underline and one restrained outer boundary, without turning each option into a filled segment or overpowering the real activity imagery.
- Protected surfaces: About and hackathon registration keep their rectilinear narrative language; hackathon outcomes keep their poster language; this brief is an Events pilot, not a sitewide repaint.
- Unresolved decisions: only tokens that survive user acceptance on this pilot may later be proposed for AI Community, Project Plaza or other public pages.
